    It is an immense distance that stands between you and everything else.

    Let us begin.

    On Neptune you take the first step, which no one will witness.
    Still invisible, you perceive with eyes closed a realm of foundational sensations. You feel what the Others think and see no need to tell it apart from what you are thinking yourself.
    Voices without sound that tickle your intuition but remain veiled by a blue mist.
    It is on Neptune that the first contact with the Flow, and all contacts, occur. It is not immediate, but always present in your every movement, action, thought.
    Silence is not Void.

    One finds a home on Uranus when decisions are made that no one shares and that only you know to be right. When you are seeing things others do not, when you are launched forward without heeding what holds you back.
    When you are in solitude, you go to the planet that spins backwards on a tilted axis.

    A pitiless martial barrier. Cold metals and gases, sharp rings. Everything that lies athwart and hinders doing, thinking, acting. You do not pass here unless you surrender. The teacher who wants to make you suffer to strengthen your temper. The rite of passage that is truly hard. All of this you will find on Saturn. But perhaps even the hardest lessons can be taught with sensitivity rather than with blows?

    The planet with the eye: Jupiter.
    An immense force, the greatest in the System, that moves silently, observing every phenomenon, every movement, every nuance.
    Command does not come through imperatives but through a few guiding hints at the right moment.
    It will not answer any question when you want it to, but if it makes its voice heard, you must listen without a word. It is the most advanced level of lurking: unostentatious omniscience.

    The will to leave nothing out and to carry everything along.
    The desire to forge one's own reality, from which one will not, once again, be cut off.
    The need to write and erase rules instead of always and only having to read and follow them.
    These are some of the principles that bring war.
    Not war understood as armies, clashes, weapons, but the state of war: the friction that accompanies our relations with the outside world. On Mars there is no hatred or cruelty, unless you bring it with you. The first thing one learns on the red planet is the acceptance of the violent component in every being, phenomenon, relationship.
    The acceptance of one's own and others' decay, oxidation, death. A difficult game that often ends in blood, because the voice of Mars is perhaps the hardest one to listen to.

    You do not choose your home, yet here you are, on Earth, under the eye of a Moon that seems unreliable but always returns. Beneath the surface of the Earth you live on, countless cores lie scattered. Do not believe they have all been discovered just because humans are now almost everywhere. The Moon knows.

    If you try to find out something about Venus, you will find only mist. The planet shrouded in mystery that force does not touch. You can understand, or be the object of understanding, only if you leave outside the mist everything you know.
    On Mars you find your way to resist and strive; on Venus, your way to let go and strip yourself of every structure.

    One does not enter Mercury without having solved the problem of excessive heat and speed of reflexes.
    No one has an advantage: strength increases the heat, surrender increases the chances of dying. So come, think fast; staying on Mercury depends on this alone.
    And when you have found the way, you will be able to observe the Sun from the last possible outpost.

    The Sun gives the energy that makes the possible possible, but it cannot be looked at directly, nor approached, nor touched.
    Though luminous and radiant, it is more unknowable than its dark opposite, Pluto.
    When a dualism ignites, it is a mistake to hope that fixed positions will be maintained.
    Death in the Sun closes the circle."
